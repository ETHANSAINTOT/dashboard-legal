Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Epitech Dashboard, a student project, handles your data.

Data we store

  • Account data: first name, last name, email address, and password (stored hashed with bcrypt).
  • Widget configuration: the widgets you add to your dashboard and their settings.
  • Google OAuth tokens: the access token and refresh token are stored in the application's PostgreSQL database. These tokens are currently not encrypted.

Gmail data

  • Emails are read through the Gmail API at the moment they are displayed.
  • Emails are not stored, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.
  • The application never modifies or deletes your emails: it only uses the read-only scope (gmail.readonly).

Your controls

  • Disconnect: detaches the Gmail widget. Access is revoked with Google when no widget uses it anymore.
  • Delete my account: deletes all of your data.
  • You can also remove the application's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Epitech Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
